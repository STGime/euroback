📉 Free-tier caps tightened — and why you don't need to worry

New Free projects created after the migration land on tighter caps: 5,000 MAU, 512 MB storage, 2 GB bandwidth, 50 realtime connections. Older shape was 10k / 1 GB / 5 GB / 100 — roughly double. New Free projects also auto-pause after 30 days idle and wake on the first request (about 30 s).

Your existing Free projects are grandfathered. The migration set grandfathered_until = now() + 90 days on every existing Free project, and the enforcement functions consult the old caps while that window is open. You'll see a small banner in the console counting down. About two weeks before switchover I'll send a personal mail so you can decide whether to upgrade to Pro or trim usage.

Pro is €19/mo per project at 100k MAU / 100 GB storage / 250 GB bandwidth / 10k realtime connections, never pauses, BYO SMTP, Slack quota alerts. Team tier (€149/mo, dedicated Postgres per project, SSO, RBAC, SOC 2) is coming soon.

📨 Onboarding drip + mail preferences

New signups now get a 6-mail welcome series over their first 10 days (Welcome → RLS → Storage + Realtime → Functions + Vault → CLI + MCP → Compliance + roadmap). You won't get these — they only fire for post-Aug 1 signups — but you'll want to know about the flip side: console → Account → Mail is a new page with a per-category opt-out toggle. That's the same lever the drip's unsubscribe link uses, and where you can silence these updates if you want.

Transactional mail from your own project (verification, password reset, magic link) is separate and always sends.
