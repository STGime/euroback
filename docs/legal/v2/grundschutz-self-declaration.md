# BSI IT-Grundschutz — Self-declaration

**Self-attested. Not externally audited.**

BSI IT-Grundschutz is the BSI's baseline for information security in organisations. External certification (ISO 27001 auf Basis von IT-Grundschutz) is only requested by public-sector and KRITIS-regulated customers. For SMB / Mittelstand customers a self-declaration against the Grundschutz-Profile for kleine und mittlere Unternehmen (SME) is the standard artifact — that's what this document is.

## Applied Grundschutz-Profile

- **Grundschutz-Profil "Basis-Absicherung nach IT-Grundschutz für kleine Unternehmen"** — the BSI's Basis-Absicherung profile targeted at small organisations (fewer than 50 employees), which describes Eurobase's current scale.
- **Grundschutz-Kompendium 2023 Edition** — reference document set.

## Self-attested control status

The Basis-Absicherung profile enumerates ~60 baseline controls across process, infrastructure, IT systems, networks/applications, and organisation. As of the effective date of this declaration:

- **Implemented:** ~85% of applicable controls.
- **In progress:** ~10%. Primarily around formal documentation (some controls are technically implemented but the process documentation is inline in code rather than in a separate operations manual).
- **Not applicable:** ~5%. Primarily physical-security controls (delegated to Scaleway) and industry-specific controls (e.g. process-control-system security).

The full control-by-control status is maintained internally and available under NDA on request.

## What this attestation is not

- Not an ISO 27001 on the basis of IT-Grundschutz certificate.
- Not an external audit.
- Not a legally binding statement — self-declarations under Grundschutz are advisory and used at the Customer's risk-management discretion.

## Path forward

The Grundschutz self-declaration is the interim artifact until formal ISO 27001 certification lands (see the ISO 27001 SoA). Customers who need a formally-attested Grundschutz certificate should raise this at engagement time so the roadmap can be adjusted.
