# NIS-2 positioning

**Where Eurobase sits under the NIS-2 Directive (Richtlinie (EU) 2022/2555) and its German implementation.**

## Eurobase's own classification

The NIS-2 Directive was transposed into German law by the NIS2-Umsetzungs- und Cybersicherheitsstärkungsgesetz (NIS2UmsuCG, in force since early 2025). It classifies organisations providing digital services into:

- **Wesentliche Einrichtungen** (essential entities) — direct high-scrutiny regulation, mandatory registration with the BSI.
- **Wichtige Einrichtungen** (important entities) — lower scrutiny, still registration + incident reporting.

Eurobase falls under **Anhang II Nr. 6 — Anbieter digitaler Dienste** (specifically: Cloud-Computing-Dienste / Managed Service Provider) once the size thresholds are crossed:

- 50+ employees, OR
- Annual turnover + balance sheet > €10 million.

**As of the effective date:** Eurobase is *below* both thresholds and therefore is NOT directly regulated under NIS-2. We are neither a "wesentliche" nor a "wichtige" Einrichtung by the size test.

**Voluntary compliance:** Even below-threshold, we operate to the NIS-2 baseline controls because our Customers who *are* regulated will cascade the requirements to us via their supplier-management processes.

## Cascaded obligations we already meet

- **Multi-factor authentication.** Available on all platform accounts.
- **Encryption in transit and at rest.** All Customer data.
- **Incident reporting SLA.** 24h to Customer / 72h to supervisory authority for breaches affecting them (Breach Register + workflow — see internal/breach + DPA v2 §10).
- **Supply-chain risk management.** Sub-processor register + annual review.
- **Vulnerability management.** GitHub Dependabot + periodic manual audits.

## When our position changes

We commit to:

- Register with the BSI within the mandated period (currently 3 months after crossing the threshold) once we cross the employee / turnover thresholds.
- Publish an updated version of this document within 30 days of registration.
- Notify all Legal Team Customers directly of the status change.
